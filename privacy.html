Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

This policy covers this website and the publishing tool that The Unfinished Amen uses to post its own content (called "the app" below). The app is a private tool. Only the owner of The Unfinished Amen uses it, to upload and schedule videos and posts to The Unfinished Amen's own accounts. It has no public users and no sign-up.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to The Unfinished Amen's own YouTube channel and to read that channel's basic details (its name and ID). It does not access viewers' data, comments, subscribers' personal information, or any other Google account.
  • Facebook, Instagram and Threads (Meta): with the owner's permission, the app publishes posts to The Unfinished Amen's own Page and profiles.

What we store and share

  • Access tokens are stored securely on the owner's own computer, plus an encrypted copy used only by the owner's private posting service to publish scheduled posts. They are never sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • We do not collect, store or share personal information about viewers, followers or visitors.
  • This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be removed at any time from your Google account's security settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is removed, the stored token stops working, and the owner deletes it from the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: send a message to The Unfinished Amen on Facebook.
